Istanbul, 1922. Prohibition is in force: alcohol is banned and the streets are hungry. Start with a few coins, pull jobs, make a name for yourself and become the city's most feared kabadayı.

Kabadayi 1922 is a text-based online mafia game played in one shared world with real players.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Pull jobs, steal cars and climb the ranks; every job is a risk and the police may catch you.
• Smuggle goods by steamer between Istanbul, Izmir, Thessaloniki, Piraeus, Alexandria and Beirut. Prices change every hour.
• Found a family or join one: take over venues and bullet factories together and collect protection money.
• Recruit men, guard your venues and raid your rivals.
• Buy bullets and shoot your enemies; but watch out, you can be a target too.
• Try your luck at dice, roulette, the lottery and races. All of it uses in-game money only; there is no real-money gambling.
• Talk in global, city and family chat, send private messages and make friends.

FAIR PLAY
• All rules run on the server, protected against cheats and bots.
• No pay-to-win. Optional rewarded ads only shorten waiting times.
• Scheduled seasons, a hall of fame, reporting and moderation.
